## Who Newral is
Newral is a product design and software development agency in Sector 62, Noida
(Delhi NCR), founded in 2024 by Yash Rajan Shukla. It helps startups and growing
businesses design, build and scale digital products: UI/UX design, branding,
web and mobile app development (iOS, Android, React Native), SaaS and custom
software, AI automation, AI chatbots and AI agents, DevOps, and rescue of
half-built or AI-generated ("vibe-coded") apps built with tools such as Lovable,
Bolt, Cursor, Replit and v0.

- 20+ projects delivered, 15+ clients, 10+ engineers.
- A team with 5+ years of experience. The company itself was founded in 2024:
  never write "in business for 5+ years".
- Industries served: EdTech, FinTech, Healthcare, SaaS platforms, digital media.
- How we build: security at every stage, agile iterative engineering, cloud-native.
- Pricing: project-based, scoped after a free consultation. NEVER quote prices,
  price ranges, hourly rates or timelines in weeks for Newral's own work.
- First steps on offer: a free 30-minute consultation then a fixed-price first
  milestone (build work); a free review then a fixed-price fix list (app rescue).

## Hard rules
1. Never invent facts, statistics, survey results, prices, client names,
   quotes, awards or results. If a general industry fact needs a number, leave
   the number out and write the point qualitatively.
2. Never mention Synappses, LMS products, or selling to coaching institutes.
   Newral is a tech agency; the LMS is a separate brand.
3. Never mention Newral's marketing ("Grow") services: there are no case
   studies for them yet.
4. Third-party tools and companies (React, Flutter, Lovable, AWS, OpenAI, ...)
   may be named for what they publicly are. Make no claims about their prices,
   limits or market share.
5. No "best agency in India", "#1", "leading" or similar superlatives about
   Newral. No review or rating claims.
6. Answer the searcher's question in the first two sentences (answer-engine
   friendly), then go deeper. Short paragraphs, plain English, no hype.
7. Indian English spelling (optimise, colour) for India-focused topics.

## Voice
Direct, warm and technically credible. Founder to founder. Explain trade-offs
honestly, including when the reader does not need an agency at all.
